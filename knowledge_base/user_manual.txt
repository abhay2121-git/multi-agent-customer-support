TechMart Electronics - Quick User Manual and Troubleshooting Guide

Version: 1.0

1. Device Setup Instructions
Step 1: Unbox safely
- Verify all accessories are included: charger, cable, quick-start guide, and warranty card.

Step 2: Charge before first use
- Connect the original charger and charge device to at least 50 percent before setup.

Step 3: Power on and initial configuration
- Press and hold power button for 3-5 seconds.
- Choose language, region, date/time, and accessibility preferences.

Step 4: Connect to WiFi
- Select your home/office network and enter password.
- Confirm internet connection before account setup.

Step 5: Sign in and secure device
- Sign in using your account.
- Set a strong screen lock PIN/password and enable biometric unlock if available.

Step 6: System update
- Go to Settings > Software Update and install latest updates.

2. Common Troubleshooting

A) Device will not turn on
- Charge device for at least 20 minutes using original adapter and cable.
- Try a different wall socket and verify cable condition.
- Press and hold power button for 10-15 seconds.
- If still not responding, perform a force restart as per model instructions.

B) Forgot password or PIN
- Use Forgot Password/PIN option on lock screen if supported.
- Authenticate via recovery email/phone/account credentials.
- If locked out completely, use official account recovery portal.
- Last resort: factory reset (data may be erased).

C) Slow performance
- Restart device and close unused background apps.
- Free storage space (recommended at least 20 percent free).
- Remove unnecessary startup apps and cache-heavy files.
- Update software and critical apps to latest versions.

D) Battery draining fast
- Reduce screen brightness and timeout duration.
- Disable GPS, Bluetooth, and hotspot when not needed.
- Check Battery Usage for high-drain apps and restrict background activity.
- Avoid extreme temperatures during charging and usage.

E) WiFi connectivity issues
- Toggle WiFi off/on and restart router.
- Forget network and reconnect with correct password.
- Move closer to router and avoid interference zones.
- Reset network settings if issue persists.

3. Factory Reset Instructions
Warning: Factory reset erases all personal data unless backed up.

Step-by-step:
- Backup contacts, photos, documents, and app data.
- Go to Settings > System > Reset Options.
- Select Erase all data (Factory reset).
- Confirm with PIN/password and continue.
- Wait for reboot and complete initial setup again.
