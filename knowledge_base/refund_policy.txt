TechMart Electronics - Refund and Return Policy

Effective Date: 08 July 2026

1. Return Window
Customers can request a return within 30 calendar days from the date of delivery for eligible products.

2. Conditions for Refund Eligibility
A refund may be approved when:
- The delivered product is damaged during transit.
- The product delivered is wrong (different model, variant, or color from order).
- The product is not as described on the product page.
- The product has a verified manufacturing defect reported within policy timelines.

3. Conditions for Return Acceptance
To qualify for a return, the product should:
- Be returned with original packaging, tags, manuals, and accessories.
- Include all bundled freebies or promotional items.
- Be free from customer-caused damage, tampering, or unauthorized repair.
- Match the product serial number recorded at dispatch.

4. Non-Refundable / Non-Returnable Items
The following items are generally non-refundable unless damaged or defective on arrival:
- Software licenses and digital activation keys.
- Gift cards and prepaid vouchers.
- Opened personal-use accessories such as earbuds or in-ear items (hygiene category).
- Products damaged due to misuse, liquid exposure, or accidental breakage.
- Items returned without mandatory components (charger, cable, box, invoice copy where required).

5. Refund Timeline
- Once return pickup is completed, quality inspection is performed within 1-2 business days.
- After approval, refund is initiated and typically credited within 5-7 business days.
- Bank processing timelines may vary by payment method and issuer.

6. Refund Modes
Refunds are processed to the original payment source:
- UPI orders: refunded to linked UPI account.
- Card orders: refunded to the same card.
- Net banking orders: refunded to the originating bank account.
- COD orders: refunded via bank transfer after account verification.

7. Step-by-Step Refund Process
Step 1: Raise a return request
- Go to My Orders in your TechMart account.
- Select the item and choose Request Return.
- Select the reason and upload proof (photos/video) if required.

Step 2: Verification by support team
- Our team verifies order history, product condition, and issue details.
- Additional information may be requested for faster resolution.

Step 3: Pickup scheduling
- If approved, reverse pickup is arranged to your registered address.
- In non-serviceable areas, customer self-ship instructions may be provided.

Step 4: Quality check at warehouse/service center
- Returned item is inspected for completeness and reported issue.
- If mismatch or policy violation is found, return may be rejected and item sent back.

Step 5: Refund initiation
- Approved cases are refunded to original payment method.
- You receive email/SMS confirmation with transaction reference.

8. Wrong Item / Missing Item in Package
Report within 48 hours of delivery. Keep package labels and unboxing proof where possible.

9. Damaged-on-Arrival Cases
Please report within 48 hours with clear photos of the outer box and product condition.

10. Partial Refund Scenarios
A partial refund may apply if returned product is missing accessories, packaging, or has signs of use not consistent with return reason.

11. Return Rejection Cases
Returns can be rejected if:
- Return request is raised after the 30-day window.
- Product is physically damaged by customer use.
- Product serial number does not match.
- Product is found to be fully functional when a false claim is made.
