TechMart Electronics - Shipping Policy

Effective Date: 08 July 2026

1. Shipping Coverage
TechMart Electronics delivers across most serviceable pin codes in India through trusted logistics partners.

2. Shipping Methods and Delivery Time
- Standard Shipping: Estimated delivery in 3-5 business days.
- Express Shipping: Estimated delivery in 1-2 business days in eligible locations.
Delivery estimates begin after order confirmation and successful payment authorization.

3. Free Shipping Policy
- Free shipping is available on orders above Rs. 2000.
- Orders below Rs. 2000 may attract a delivery fee based on destination and shipment type.
- Promotional campaigns may temporarily alter shipping charges.

4. Order Processing Timeline
- Most orders are processed within 24 hours on working days.
- Orders placed after cutoff time or on Sundays/public holidays are processed next working day.
- High-demand sale periods may increase processing time.

5. Tracking Information Process
- Once shipped, customers receive tracking details by email and SMS.
- Tracking is also visible in My Orders on the TechMart account dashboard.
- Tracking status may take up to 12 hours to reflect after dispatch.

6. Delivery Attempts
- Logistics partner may make up to three delivery attempts.
- If delivery fails due to customer unavailability or incorrect address, shipment may be returned to origin.

7. Address Accuracy and Changes
- Customers must provide accurate address and contact details during checkout.
- Address updates are possible only before shipment is dispatched.

8. Undelivered Package Policy
If your package is marked undelivered, returned, or lost in transit:
- Contact support within 48 hours of status update.
- We will coordinate with courier partner for investigation.
- If package is confirmed lost or undeliverable, replacement or full refund will be offered as per eligibility.

9. Delayed Shipment Policy
Delivery delays may occur due to weather, strikes, remote-area constraints, or unforeseen logistics issues. Customers will be notified through order updates where possible.

10. Damaged Package at Delivery
If the outer package appears tampered or damaged:
- Record unboxing video if possible.
- Note damage to delivery agent (where available).
- Report issue to support within 48 hours with evidence.

11. International Shipping
Currently, TechMart Electronics does not support international deliveries unless explicitly announced.
