TechMart Electronics - Warranty Policy

Effective Date: 08 July 2026

1. Standard Warranty Coverage
Most eligible products sold by TechMart Electronics include a 1 year standard warranty from the date of delivery, unless otherwise stated on the product page or invoice.

2. What is Covered
Warranty typically covers:
- Manufacturing defects in hardware components.
- Functional failure under normal usage conditions.
- Internal electrical faults not caused by external damage.

3. What is Not Covered
Warranty does not cover:
- Physical damage such as cracks, dents, or broken screens.
- Water or liquid damage.
- Damage caused by power surges or improper voltage use.
- Unauthorized repair, disassembly, or software modification.
- Consumables and normal wear-and-tear items.
- Cosmetic issues that do not affect product functionality.

4. Warranty Claim Eligibility
To submit a valid warranty claim, customer should provide:
- Original purchase invoice from TechMart Electronics.
- Product serial number/IMEI that matches invoice records.
- Issue description and, where possible, photo/video evidence.

5. How to Claim Warranty
Step 1: Contact support
- Email support@techmart.in or call 1800-XXX-XXXX.
- Share order ID, product details, and issue summary.

Step 2: Remote troubleshooting
- Our team may perform basic diagnostics remotely.
- If unresolved, a service request is raised.

Step 3: Service center routing
- Product is directed to authorized service center.
- Pickup/drop instructions are provided based on category and location.

Step 4: Inspection and resolution
- Technician verifies fault and warranty eligibility.
- Repair, replacement, or alternative remedy is provided accordingly.

6. Turnaround Time
Typical service resolution ranges from 5 to 15 business days depending on product type, parts availability, and service center load.

7. Dead-on-Arrival (DOA) Cases
Products found defective immediately on delivery should be reported within 48 hours. DOA handling may qualify for faster replacement subject to verification.

8. Extended Warranty Options
TechMart offers paid extended warranty plans on select categories:
- Extension up to additional 1 or 2 years.
- May include priority support and doorstep service for specific products.
- Plan activation conditions and exclusions apply.

9. Important Notes
- Data backup is customer responsibility before handing over devices for service.
- Warranty terms may vary by brand and product category.
- Final warranty decision is based on authorized technician assessment and policy terms.
