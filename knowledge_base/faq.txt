TechMart Electronics - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

We provide smartphones, laptops, tablets, audio accessories, smart home products, and computer peripherals for students, professionals, and families. Our mission is to make technology accessible with transparent pricing, genuine products, and responsive customer care.

Frequently Asked Questions

1) How can I place an order on TechMart Electronics?
You can place an order by browsing products on our website, adding items to your cart, and completing checkout using your preferred payment method. You will receive an order confirmation email once payment is successful.

2) Do I need to create an account to buy products?
Yes, creating an account helps us provide order tracking, invoice history, warranty support, and faster checkouts.

3) How do I track my order?
After your order is dispatched, you will receive an email and SMS with tracking details. You can also track your order from the Orders section in your TechMart account.

4) What payment methods do you accept?
We accept UPI, debit cards, credit cards, net banking, wallets, and selected EMI options through partner banks.

5) Can I cancel my order after placing it?
Yes, you can cancel an order before it is packed for shipment. Once packed, cancellation may not be possible, but you can request a return after delivery if eligible.

6) Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?
COD is available for select pin codes and products. The option will appear at checkout if your order is eligible.

7) How long does delivery usually take?
Standard shipping generally takes 3-5 business days, while express shipping takes 1-2 business days in supported areas.

8) Is shipping free?
Shipping is free for orders above Rs. 2000. Orders below this threshold may include a shipping fee based on location and shipment type.

9) What if I receive a damaged or defective item?
Please report the issue within 48 hours of delivery with photos or an unboxing video. Our support team will verify the case and guide you through replacement or refund options.

10) How can I return a product?
You can raise a return request from your account under Orders, or contact support@techmart.in. Returns are accepted within 30 days for eligible products.

11) How long does it take to get a refund?
After return pickup and quality checks, refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method.

12) Are all products covered under warranty?
Most electronics include a manufacturer or seller warranty. Warranty duration and terms are listed on each product page and invoice.

13) What is not covered under warranty?
Warranty usually does not cover accidental breakage, liquid damage, unauthorized repairs, misuse, cosmetic wear, or normal battery aging.

14) Can I buy extended warranty?
Yes, extended warranty plans are available on select devices at checkout or within a limited period after purchase.

15) How do I contact customer support quickly?
For fastest support, call 1800-XXX-XXXX during business hours. You may also email support@techmart.in with your order ID and issue details.

16) I forgot my account password. What should I do?
Use the Forgot Password option on the login page and follow the OTP or email reset instructions.

17) Do prices include GST?
Yes, all listed prices are inclusive of applicable taxes unless explicitly stated otherwise.

18) Can I get a GST invoice for business purchases?
Yes, please enter correct GST details during checkout to generate a GST-compliant invoice.

19) Do you match competitor pricing?
We offer a conditional price match policy on identical models sold by authorized retailers. Documentation and eligibility checks are required.

20) Can I change my delivery address after order placement?
Address changes may be possible before dispatch. Contact support as soon as possible with your order details.

21) What should I do if tracking shows delivered but I have not received the package?
Please contact support within 24 hours of status update. We will coordinate with the logistics partner for proof of delivery and resolution.

22) Is my payment information secure?
Yes, payments are processed through secure, PCI-compliant partners. TechMart does not store full card details on its servers.

23) Do you provide installation assistance for devices?
Setup guides are provided with products, and remote support is available for select categories.

24) How can I escalate a support issue?
If unresolved, reply to your ticket email with the subject Escalation Request and include prior ticket references. A senior specialist will review the case.
